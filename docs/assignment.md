# Вимоги викладача

Джерело: завдання, надане користувачем у чаті. Технічні плани в цьому репозиторії не розширюють критерії оцінювання.

> Build a working prototype of the product from your one-pager, put it in front of real users and report what happened. The demo is shown on **Thursday 19 November (Module 11)**.
>
> **Submit:** in the online text, the link to your repository and the URL of the running demo; as a file, the report (PDF). Every team member submits.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| The repository is organised properly, with these top-level parts: `backend/`, `frontend/`, `infra/` and a `README.md` that tells a stranger what the product is and how to run and deploy it | 3 |
| A working prototype with sign-in — deployed, reachable over HTTPS, users can sign up and sign in | 3 |
| A report with user feedback — who you showed it to, how many people tried it, what they said, and what you changed because of it | 4 |

**Bonus:** Extra points if the users you brought in were ready to pay for the product with money: a donation to a volunteer cause, or a working payment in the product. Show the evidence in the report.

Календар планування: 19 листопада 2026, Europe/Kyiv. Донавчання ШІ, додаткові методи чи матеріали OOAD не є окремими критеріями цього завдання.
